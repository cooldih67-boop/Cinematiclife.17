About Me

A little about the filmmaker behind the camera.

John Velasco

@cinematiclife.17

Hi there! I’m a cinematography driven filmmaker who enjoys shooting life's daily moments with a cinematic flair. I produce visually driven content that focuses around intentional composition, movement, lighting and editing.

Some of my favorite things to shoot are cinematic lifestyle, sports /action, events, environmental art and filmmaking BTS. I love trying new things and shooting in unique ways that make each moment feel larger than life and cinematic.

My goal is to push creativity and tell stories through my work that are captivating and leave a lasting impression.
